<h4><strong>Trips for Internationalisation Experience: Hanoi</strong></h4>
<p>On 24 March 2026, 38 students and 5 teachers embarked on an Internationalisation
Trip to Hanoi, Vietnam. The programme was crafted to extend learning beyond
the classroom, centring on education, culture, and innovation. Through
this experience, students were challenged to step outside their comfort
zone and develop a broader global perspective.</p>
<p>One of the highlights of the trip was visiting two schools: Cau Giay High
School and Alpha School. From the moment we arrived, we were greeted by
the warm hospitality of the students. We had the opportunity to sit in
their classes, try authentic Vietnamese snacks, and engage in meaningful
conversations with the students. Despite the language barriers, our Crescentians
found common ground with their Vietnamese peers, gaining valuable insights
into Vietnam’s education system and culture. They also managed to make
new friends and exchange contact numbers, making the experience even more
meaningful.</p>
<p>We also visited various companies such as KymViet and the Singapore Embassy
in Vietnam. At KymViet, we learned how people with disabilities earn a
living through crafting handmade items such as keychains and plush toys.
We purchase all sorts of good quality and cute souvenirs. Visits to FPT
Hanoi and Vietnam Singapore Industrial Parks (VSIP), provided us with a
glimpse of Vietnam’s rapidly growing innovation landscape, and how the
next generation is shaping innovation and progress.</p>
<p>Overall, this journey was not just about exploring a new country, but
also about building confidence, deepening cultural awareness, and creating
lifelong memories.</p>
