<h4><strong>Secondary 1 Outdoor Adventure Learning Camp</strong></h4>
<p>On 25 March, the Secondary 1 students from Crescent Girls’ School arrived
at Sarimbun Camp filled with anticipation and excitement. We were warmly
welcomed by the enthusiastic and cheerful facilitators and camp chiefs,
who introduced themselves through lively and engaging performances. In
no time, we became well-acquainted with them and were energised to begin
the camp activities.</p>
<p>Throughout the camp, we took part in a wide range of activities, including
high elements, abseiling, and ziplining. Many courageous students stepped
forward to volunteer first, setting inspiring examples for their peers.
One particularly memorable experience for most of the Secondary 1 students
was the water confidence activity, affectionately nicknamed the “milo pond.”
Wearing flotation devices, we entered the pond in groups and completed
various challenges, such as floating on our backs. Laughter filled the
air as we linked arms and attempted to stay afloat while singing our school
song together.</p>
<p>In addition, staying overnight in tents and dormitories introduced us
to new and unfamiliar environments, allowing us to grow more independent
and adaptable. The campfire night brought the experience to a perfect close,
with lively performances by each class and energetic dances led by our
facilitators.</p>
<p>All the Secondary 1 students returned home with countless stories to share,
valuable lessons to carry forward, and unforgettable memories that will
last a lifetime.</p>
