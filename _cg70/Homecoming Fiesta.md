<p>It’s a Crescentian reunion!</p>
<p>CGS is turning 70, and we are throwing a Homecoming Fiesta to celebrate.</p>

<br>357 Tanglin Road, Crescent Girls’ School</p>
<p>Open to all! No registration required.</p>
<p>See you there!</p>
